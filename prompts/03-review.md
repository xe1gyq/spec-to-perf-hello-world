# Prompt 3 — Review evidence

Compare the implementation and tests with each acceptance criterion in
specs/001-matrix-scan.md. Inspect argument bounds, allocation size, indexing,
integer overflow, and compiler optimization effects. Distinguish correctness,
wall-clock observations, and hardware-counter evidence. Identify any acceptance
criterion not verified. Propose one small next experiment without silently
changing the specification. Do not fabricate a speedup or perf result.
